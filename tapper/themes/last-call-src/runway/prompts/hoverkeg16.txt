Three copies of one object side by side in a row, for a pixel art video game in the 16-bit console style of the early 1990s, evenly spaced with wide flat pure magenta space between them. The object is a hovering drinks barrel on a space station. The picture @old is a very simple 8-bit drawing of it, a floating grey pod with a red mark; redraw it as a proper upright steel drinks barrel with the same shape and material as the barrel in @barrel: brushed steel with rolled ribs, a rolled rim at the top and the bottom, dents and scratches, and a small orange five-pointed star on its front. Under the barrel is bolted a ring of three small rocket thrusters, and each thruster fires a bright blue jet of flame straight down that keeps the barrel hovering: white-hot at the nozzle, then cyan, then deep blue at the tip. The three copies are exactly the same barrel in exactly the same place and size; only the blue jets differ: in the first they are short, in the second medium, in the third long, as three frames of a flickering flame. Each copy is seen from the front and slightly above. Fine small pixels, smooth painted shading in four or five close tones, clean dark outlines, small sharp highlights, light from the upper left. No shadow, no floor, no people, no text.
