A landscape painting for a pixel art video game in the 16-bit console style of the early 1990s. It fills the whole picture edge to edge: no frame, no border, no margin. A night camp on the old American frontier: a deep blue sky with a thin moon and stars, a canvas-topped wagon on the left, a campfire on the right with sparks rising, saddles and a coffee pot lit orange beside it, dark trees behind. Night, firelight. Midnight blue, fire orange, ember red, warm ochre. Grand light and deep space, painted like an oil landscape but in pixels. Fine small pixels, smooth shading in four or five close tones, clean dark brown outlines, small sharp highlights. No speckle, no noise, no blur, no text, no signature.
