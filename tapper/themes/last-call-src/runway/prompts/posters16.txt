Two separate gig posters side by side, for a pixel art video game in the 16-bit console style of the early 1990s, each a tall upright rectangle seen flat from the front, with wide flat pure magenta space between them and around them.
Left poster: a heavy metal gig poster. A grinning skull with a tall spiked green mohawk and crossed lightning bolts behind it, on a black background with a blood-red border, and the made-up band name RUST HOUNDS in jagged sharp silver letters across the top.
Right poster: a punk gig poster in a cut-and-paste photocopy style. A clenched fist holding up a foaming mug, in hot pink and acid yellow on torn black and white checkers, with the made-up band name THE SPILLS in ransom-note letters across the top, and a strip of tape at each top corner.
Each poster is about twice as tall as it is wide, with slightly torn and curled paper edges. Big bold simple shapes that read from far away, flat bold colours, clean hard dark outlines, fine small pixels. No other text, no people, no wall.
