The picture @tunnel is pixel art of the side of a tunnel on a military science fiction base: a row of heavy steel ribs, each leaning back at the top and then running straight down, and at the right the hazard-striped frame of the tunnel's opening. Right now the spaces between the ribs are empty, which makes the tunnel look unfinished. Redraw the same picture, keeping every rib, the frame, their sizes, their places and their angles exactly as they are, and fill each space between two ribs with the tunnel's wall: recessed dark armoured panels set well back behind the ribs, in deep blue-grey steel, with seams, rivets, a few thin pipes and cables, small vents, and shadow cast on them by the rib to their left, so the ribs stand out in front as raised frames and the tunnel reads as one solid structure with real depth. The wall follows the ribs: it leans back at the top where they lean and stops where they stop. Nothing is added outside the outline of the ribs and the frame. Same 16-bit console pixel art style of the early 1990s, fine small pixels, smooth painted shading, clean dark outlines, light from the upper left. Flat pure magenta background, no people, no text.
