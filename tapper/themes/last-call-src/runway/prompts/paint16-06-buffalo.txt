A landscape painting for a pixel art video game in the 16-bit console style of the early 1990s. It fills the whole picture edge to edge: no frame, no border, no margin. Buffalo crossing a river on the old American frontier: pale bluffs under a pale sky, a dark herd pouring down a cut bank into a wide brown river, heads and backs splashing through the water toward the viewer, white spray. Overcast morning light. Grey-blue, buff, dark brown, khaki, white. Grand light and deep space, painted like an oil landscape but in pixels. Fine small pixels, smooth shading in four or five close tones, clean dark brown outlines, small sharp highlights. No speckle, no noise, no blur, no text, no signature.
